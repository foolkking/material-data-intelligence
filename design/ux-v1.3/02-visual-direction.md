# Visual Direction: Scientific Research Workbench

## Art direction

The product should feel like a calm scientific instrument with the reading hierarchy of a paper and the spatial discipline of a desktop workbench. It is compact but not cramped, technical but not infrastructural, and authoritative without visual theater.

The visual signature is the **evidence spine**: a restrained vertical sequence which connects resolved data, scientific method, artifact, finding, limitation and evidence. It appears in the landing workbench scene, running Activity, results, and Inspector lineage. It gives the product a recognizable research character without decorative science imagery.

## Composition rules

- Landing uses a product workbench scene as the visual asset. The next content band remains visible at the desktop fold.
- Console/Overview are unframed content bands with hairline separators and a limited number of dense, scan-friendly rows.
- Data and Research become fixed workbenches below the global/local bars. The primary object gets the largest region.
- Reports use a central `820-900px` scientific reading measure.
- Green identifies the active action/object/step only. It is never a general surface color.
- Product chrome is Simplified Chinese. Scientific identifiers retain their canonical/source form only where they carry material-science meaning.
- Running shares focus between observable work and an emerging artifact; Complete returns focus to the scientific result and collapses process history by default.

## Why this direction is correct for the product

It reinforces the frozen model: a researcher brings data into a Project, asks a research question, observes the actual work, and reads a supported scientific result. It rejects the current baseline failure where Dataset/Profile/Provider/Plan/Job/Queue UI competes with the user’s research question.

## Deliberate exclusions

- No generic AI glow, purple gradient, glass, bento widget grid, decorative molecule art, large SaaS radius, or gratuitous KPI cards.
- No chatbot-bubble-led primary interface. Conversation is a work pane, not the entire application.
- No one-size-fits-all viewer card. Structure, table, spectra, trajectory, volumetric data, archive detection, and unsupported files have distinct spatial compositions.
