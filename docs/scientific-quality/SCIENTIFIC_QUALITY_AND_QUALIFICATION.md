# Scientific Quality and Qualification

Status: CURRENT  
Evidence contract: `1.0`  
Canonical command: `uv run python scripts/scientific_quality_report.py --check`

## Purpose

The Capability extension validator proves that a Capability is registered,
executable, project-safe and connected to its Tool, Adapter, Artifact ports,
ScientificResult projector and optional presentation surfaces. It does **not**
prove that the scientific method is correct.

Scientific qualification is a separate, evidence-backed property:

```text
EXTENSION_CONTRACT_PASS != SCIENTIFIC_QUALITY_PASS
GROUNDING_PASS != GOOD_SCIENTIFIC_INTERPRETATION
```

The canonical evidence records live in
`mdi_api.research_agent.capabilities.quality_evidence`. Generated JSON and
Markdown inventories under `docs/scientific-quality/generated/` are
projections and must not be edited by hand.

## Qualification levels

| Level | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `STRUCTURAL_ONLY` | Integration is coherent; no scientific quality promotion is claimed. |
| `REFERENCE_VALIDATED` | At least one analytical, independent, published, curated, or exact synthetic reference supports the declared semantics. |
| `DOMAIN_VALIDATED` | Reference evidence plus meaningful invariants and negative/applicability boundaries cover the declared domain. |
| `CROSS_IMPLEMENTATION_VALIDATED` | An actually independent implementation agrees under a documented comparison policy. |
| `EXTERNAL_IMPLEMENTATION_SEMANTICS_VALIDATED` | This platform's parsing, units, conventions, Artifact/Result projection and limitations are validated, but the upstream library/solver is not independently qualified here. |
| `NOT_YET_SCIENTIFICALLY_QUALIFIED` | The Capability is explicitly under-qualified. |

Historical `SCIENTIFICALLY_QUALIFIED`, `EXPERIMENTAL`, and `UNKNOWN` values
remain backward-readable. New promotions use the more precise levels above.
No level means universal scientific correctness; every level is bounded by
its applicability and limitations.

## Evidence layers

The evidence model keeps distinct links for:

- computational/reference correctness;
- scientific invariants;
- negative and applicability-domain behavior;
- Adapter and Artifact semantics;
- Artifact to ScientificResult projection;
- interpretation grounding and epistemic restraint;
- Report fidelity and limitation preservation;
- real integration paths;
- regression-only stability.

Regression output generated by the same implementation is useful but cannot,
by itself, promote scientific qualification.

## Reference authority

Numeric expectations record an origin, method/version, unit and justified
tolerance. Accepted reference classes are:

- analytical or hand-computable;
- independent implementation;
- published reference;
- curated known result with recorded provenance;
- exact synthetic fixture;
- official-library or external-solver semantics, explicitly scoped to the
  platform-owned integration boundary;
- regression-only, which never acts as independent ground truth.

Phase 11 golden replay remains supporting identity/invariant evidence. Its
eight `PASS_INVARIANT` and four reference-limited records are not silently
relabelled as numeric reference validation.

## Tolerances, units and conventions

There is no global epsilon. Each numeric evidence link declares an absolute,
relative, or domain-specific bound and explains why it is appropriate.
Current examples include:

- XRD matching: `0.15 degree` in 2-theta;
- trajectory timestep and synthetic MSD: tight analytical floating-point
  bounds;
- diffusion: explicit fit-regime constraints (`R2` and alpha) plus
  quantity-specific coefficient tolerances;
- electronic energies: `1e-8 eV` for supplied semantic fixtures;
- phonon near-zero and density conversion: producer/conversion-specific
  bounds;
- tabular/PCA/ML metrics: analytical finite-array tolerances.

Units and conventions are part of correctness. Evidence covers angstrom,
angstrom squared, picosecond, electronvolt, terahertz, states-per-energy,
degree, fractions, relative intensity and dimensionless quantities. Coordinate
mode, periodic wrapping, timestep, normalization, fit window, broadening and
source-qualified classification remain explicit where relevant.

## Applicability and limitations

Eligibility answers whether a Capability can run. Qualification answers how
well its behavior has been demonstrated. Planning remains a separate choice
among eligible Capabilities.

Important unsupported inputs must fail explicitly rather than emit meaningless
numbers. Current evidence includes non-periodic structures, disordered
coordination input, incompatible XRD wavelength, ambiguous trajectory unwrap,
non-diffusive fit regimes, mixed Dataset units, insufficient PCA rank,
unsupported ML uncertainty, malformed electronic resources and mismatched
phonon source identities.

Limitations are included in the bounded planner-facing quality projection, but
test paths and internal evidence machinery are not exposed to the model.

## Version awareness

Each quality record binds to a SHA-256 fingerprint of the complete scientific
Capability contract, including:

- scientific purpose and requirements;
- exact input/output semantics;
- Tool binding and parameter mapping;
- Result/projector and Viewer bindings;
- resource/recovery policy and presentation support.

The qualification summary itself is excluded, preventing a self-referential
hash. A scientific contract change without evidence review produces
`STALE_QUALIFICATION`; the old evidence is retained but cannot remain current
silently.

## Current qualification inventory

The current 17 Capability records are honestly mixed:

- `DOMAIN_VALIDATED` (6): trajectory RDF/MSD/diffusion, Dataset Materials
  Explorer, Composition Space, and ML regression evaluation.
- `REFERENCE_VALIDATED` (3): structure RDF, distance-cutoff coordination
  histogram, and experimental/theoretical XRD comparison.
- `EXTERNAL_IMPLEMENTATION_SEMANTICS_VALIDATED` (8): CrystalNN, VoronoiNN,
  theoretical XRD, electronic band/DOS, and phonon band/DOS/combined
  composition.

For electronic and phonon inputs the repository qualifies parsing, units,
composition and projection of supplied solver output; it does not pretend to
qualify the upstream DFT or force-constant solver. For CrystalNN, VoronoiNN and
theoretical XRD it validates the canonical Adapter contract around the locked
pymatgen implementation rather than claiming an independent reimplementation.

Exact per-Capability references, applicability, limitations, units and
fingerprints are in the generated inventory.

## Interpretation, Reports and Viewers

Deterministic interpretation checks establish:

- cited values and source identities exist in ScientificResult;
- units remain authoritative;
- partial Results remain limited;
- unsupported scientific qualifiers are rejected after bounded repair.

Report evaluation separately checks exact source/Result identity, numeric and
unit fidelity, evidence, methods, limitations, partial-state disclosure and
grounding. It is not a prose-beauty score.

Viewer semantic evidence remains in the existing viewer and frontend contract
tests: exact ScientificObjectVersion reopening, bounded payload projection,
XRD series/peak units, trajectory frame/time semantics, electronic energy
axes, and phonon shared-frequency/DOS-density conventions. Workstream D does
not redesign viewers.

## Reproducibility

Capability evidence declares `DETERMINISTIC`, `SEEDED_STOCHASTIC`,
`NONDETERMINISTIC_EXTERNAL`, or `UNKNOWN`. Deterministic scientific methods
must reproduce within their declared numerical policy. ML evaluation is
classified `SEEDED_STOCHASTIC`: authoritative metrics are deterministic for a
fixed paired evaluation payload, while upstream model training may require a
controlled seed and is outside that Capability's authority.

## Known limitations

- The inventory does not claim universal validity outside each declared
  applicability domain.
- External scientific solvers/libraries are not independently revalidated by
  wrappers that call the same implementation.
- The bounded interpretation suite establishes fidelity and restraint, not
  expert-level scientific novelty or insight.
- Report evaluation establishes source-correct scientific communication
  foundations, not final editorial quality.
- Performance, large-scale statistics and multi-hour autonomous research
  remain separate qualification problems.
