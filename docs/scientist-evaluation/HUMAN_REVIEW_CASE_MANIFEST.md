# Human Review Case Manifest

The machine-readable source is
`generated/human_review_cases.json`; it is the administrator-side manifest
and must not be distributed as a reviewer answer key.

## Formal composition

- 12 single-domain cases: trajectory/transport, structure/coordination, XRD,
  materials ML/composition, electronic structure and phonon.
- 8 cross-evidence cases: transport/structure, ML/composition,
  XRD/literature, electronic/literature, phonon/method, internal/published
  comparison, conflicting literature and review/primary lineage.
- 4 insufficient-evidence cases: causal restraint, missing baseline, bounded
  literature absence and cluster generalization.

Eight cases are anchors and receive three assignments. Normal cases receive
two assignments. Assignment balancing gives each of four reviewer slots
fourteen formal records.

## Case authority

Every case stores required/allowed/forbidden semantic boundaries, required
qualifiers and limitations, method expectations, valid stop outcomes and
evidence origin. These are repository-owned evaluation expectations, not
generated by the provider under review.

All current cases are marked
`INTERNALLY_AUTHORED_HELD_OUT_SANITIZED_FIXTURE`. The manifest makes no claim
of independent case contribution. Eight future contributor slots are included
for later use and do not count toward the current 24.

## Contamination control

The Workstream I 27-case corpus remains in
`docs/scientific-reasoning/fixtures/` and is excluded from J scoring. J cases
use new source configurations, values, combinations, questions and failure
temptations; they are not renamed I fixtures.
