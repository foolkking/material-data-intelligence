## Main findings

- Existing structure parsing, structure summaries, static structure viewing, distance-
  cutoff coordination histogram, theoretical XRD, static RDF, trajectory import/view,
  phonon band/DOS, BZ, volumetric parsing/viewing, Materials ML and composition tools
  are reusable foundations or bounded products, not proof of N1-N5 professional
  capability.
- The existing coordination histogram explicitly reports distance-cutoff sensitivity
  and no advanced environment classification. It must remain a foundation and may not
  be relabeled CrystalNN/VoronoiNN output.
- The current trajectory contract is bounded and supports identity, frames, lattice and
  time metadata, but there is no RDF/MSD/diffusion Adapter or Artifact authority.
- The renderer registry is strict and inert. It has no electronic Band/DOS renderer
  contract and no experimental XRD comparison contract.
- Current `DataProfile` is contract version `2.0` with structure, trajectory, phonon,
  volumetric, semantic-resource and readiness fields. N0 proposes additive readiness
  facts for future phases; it does not change the implementation.
- Generic Artifact persistence and Report/Recipe composition are reusable. Scientific
  numeric values must still be generated only by a future registered Adapter.
- `phonopy` and `seekpath` are not top-level locked dependencies. Their existing use is
  represented by bounded wrappers/source formats and must not be treated as installed
  dependency authority.
