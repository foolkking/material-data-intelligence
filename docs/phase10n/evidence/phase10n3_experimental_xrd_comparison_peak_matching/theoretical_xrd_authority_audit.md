# Theoretical XRD Authority Audit

`structure.xrd@0.1.0`, `XrdPatternAdapter` and `phase10e4.xrd_pattern.v1` are the existing persisted authority. It records exact wavelength, peak positions/intensities/d-spacings, structure identity and hkl/multiplicity metadata. N3 consumes this Artifact/checksum through an AnalysisPlan 0.2 port and does not calculate theoretical diffraction.
