# Phase 10N-1 Workspace And Interpretation

The Workspace gallery dispatches by exact artifact contract and version. The
coordination result surface provides algorithm switching, per-site results, neighbor
relations, exact periodic images, Inspector metadata, warnings and a non-canvas table
alternative. It does not calculate neighbors.

Grounded interpretation projects bounded algorithm, coverage, coordination range,
distance range and warnings. It does not receive an unbounded neighbor payload and
cannot invent bonds, oxidation states or a preferred algorithm. Reports include
algorithm/version, selected rows, coverage, limitations and provenance. Recipes record
the resolved parameters and source/artifact hashes and remain declarative and
non-executable.

Heavy viewer lifecycle rules remain unchanged. Coordination is metadata/table-first;
inactive payloads are not prefetched and stale responses are not committed.
