# Phase 10N-5 DOS Projection Contract

`electronic.dos@0.1.0` emits `phase10n5.electronic_dos.v1`. Total DOS must be supplied.
DOS unit semantic is `states_per_ev`, `states_per_ev_cell`,
`states_per_ev_formula_unit`, or `source_relative`. Formal element/orbital projections
stay distinct and declare `COMPLETE`, `PARTIAL`, or `UNKNOWN`. No projection summation,
interpolation, smoothing, missing-channel generation or projected-band inference occurs.
