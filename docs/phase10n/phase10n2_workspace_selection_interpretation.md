# Phase 10N-2 Workspace, Selection And Interpretation

The Workspace renderer is metadata/table-first and shows per-site environment status,
reference scores, persisted vertices/faces, metrics, source N1 provenance and limitations.
Structure display is a persisted-data overlay; tables and text remain the numeric and
accessible authority.

`WorkspaceSelectionContext 1.0` additively supports `LOCAL_ENVIRONMENT`,
`COORDINATION_POLYHEDRON`, `POLYHEDRON_VERTEX`, and `POLYHEDRON_FACE`. Every N2
selection binds the N2 Artifact/checksum, exact source N1 Artifact/checksum, immutable
structure hash, site identity, and the persisted environment, polyhedron, vertex, or face
identity required by its kind. N1/N2 site navigation requires identical
structure/site/source lineage; vertex and face identities are never reconstructed from a
screen index, table row, or proximity.

The deterministic projector exposes only bounded counts, scores, metrics, coverage,
warnings and selected-site facts. Reports compose persisted values. Recipes record exact
N1/N2 bindings and remain non-executable. No chemistry, bonding, oxidation, hybridization
or stability claim is generated.
