# Phase 12-1 Visual Language

The visual direction is **Scientific Instrument + Modern Data Workspace**:
restrained, calm, technical, high-density and low-noise. Neutral workspace
surfaces, subtle borders and one product accent establish hierarchy. Shadows are
reserved for overlays; ordinary panels use surface and divider separation rather
than nested floating cards.

Status colors are presentation aids, never a replacement for labels or a claim of
scientific validation. Product support, scientific validation, reproducibility and
presentation validation remain visibly and semantically separate. Scientific trace
and category colors are outside DS2 and are preserved for Phase 12-3.
