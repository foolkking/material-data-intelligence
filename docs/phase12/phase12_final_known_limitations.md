# Phase 12 Final Known Limitations

- Accessibility meets the repository's WCAG-like AA engineering target; no
  formal accessibility certification or real assistive-technology study is
  claimed.
- Automated journeys are engineering replays, not human usability research.
- Cross-browser validation targets semantic and layout consistency, not pixel
  equality.
- Browser, screenshot, service and accessibility evidence are not independent
  scientific validation.
- Unsupported inputs and Phase 11 scientific limits remain explicit.
- Heavy scientific viewers remain active-only and provide structured or table
  alternatives; WebGL and Plotly remain presentation-only.
