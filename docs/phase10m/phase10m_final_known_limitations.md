# Phase 10M Final Known Limitations

- One Workspace belongs to one Job; there is no multi-Job or cross-Workspace aggregation.
- There is no cross-Workspace selection, collaboration, coauthoring, or automatic merge.
- There is no offline-first authority or local canonical Workspace backup.
- Unfinalized Report drafts are session-only and intentionally unrecoverable.
- There is no generic workflow editor, arbitrary DAG, runtime replanning, or automatic rerun.
- Recipe is declarative and non-executable.
- The frontend and Report layer do not calculate or reinterpret science.
- Historical records may be read-only and may lack Profile, interpretation, or modern renderer metadata.
- Missing, tampered, and stale sources remain unavailable rather than being rebound.
- Phase 10N professional scientific capabilities are future reviewer-gated scope.
