# Future Scope

Status: CURRENT, NON-BLOCKING, NOT QUEUED

These capabilities are relevant to the domain but are not required for the
Initial Complete Product. They may enter `TASKS.md` only after explicit
reviewer/user approval.

## Advanced Scientific Visualization

* Fermi Surface from a validated three-dimensional reciprocal-space mesh,
  including iso-energy extraction, Fermi sheets, BZ clipping, spin, and picking.
* Arbitrary oblique or curved volumetric slices, vector/complex fields,
  wavefunction phase, orbital reconstruction, phase-colored surfaces, 4D data,
  sparse/octree volume, and advanced segmentation.
* ELF basins, attractors, topology, basin populations, Bader analysis, and
  charge partitioning.
* Vacuum-level detection, work function, Fermi/band/core-level alignment,
  charged-defect corrections, dipole corrections, and electric-field products.

## Advanced Structure Science

* Bond valence and oxidation-aware advanced bonding.
* Defects, vacancies, substitutions, interstitials, and environment comparison.
* Surfaces, slabs, surface Brillouin zones, and adsorption-site analysis.
* Structure editing and persisted derived edited structures.

## Advanced Experimental Analysis

* Full or multiphase Rietveld refinement.
* Structural, preferred-orientation, and instrument refinement.
* Pair distribution function and neutron/scattering extensions.

## Advanced Dynamics

* Reactive and variable-atom-count trajectories.
* Bond event and reaction-network analysis.
* Advanced diffusion models, long-trajectory streaming, and production video.

## External Scientific Compute

* DFT/MD workflow connectors, VASP, Quantum ESPRESSO, LAMMPS, remote HPC, and
  external scientific APIs.

The initial platform analyzes existing data; it does not run large simulations.

## Optional AI Extensions

* Retrieval over scientific documents, project knowledge retrieval, richer plan
  repair, long-term conversational context, and advanced agent memory.

## Optional Safe Code Execution

* Sandboxed notebooks, scripts, and user-defined analyses may be reconsidered
  only after a separately reviewed strict sandbox exists.

Tool Registry plus validated Adapters remain the execution authority.
