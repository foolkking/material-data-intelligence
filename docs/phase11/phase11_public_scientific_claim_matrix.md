# Phase 11 Public Scientific Claim Matrix

## Approved

- The current Tool, input, and official-example coverage was classified against retained evidence.
- Runtime, artifact, browser, and service evidence are reported on their own axes.
- Phase 11-2 invariant and Phase 11-3 cross-platform limitations remain explicit.

## Required qualifiers

- `derived from supplied data`
- `under the stated algorithm, tolerance, and input contract`
- `coverage classified from retained evidence`
- `numeric cross-platform comparison not applicable where the sealed record says so`

## Prohibited

Do not claim that all Tools are independently scientifically validated, that all advertised file types are
directly supported, or that all scientific outputs are numerically cross-platform verified. Do not use
browser, screenshot, service, fixture, or mapping evidence as a substitute for a scientific reference.
