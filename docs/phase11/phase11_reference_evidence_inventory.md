Identical fixture copies or transformed descendants are not independent references. Authority is determined by source and normalized hashes, transformation history, scientific contract, expected values, tolerance, and review status.

N1-N5 retain controlled positive and negative fixtures. N4 includes analytic motion and random-walk invariants. N1 library comparisons include same-library self-reference and are not independent cross-validation. N5 validates supplied electronic resources; it does not validate platform DFT. Earlier phonon, Brillouin-zone, volumetric, composition, statistics, and materials-ML evidence remains in scope for Phase 11 reconciliation.
