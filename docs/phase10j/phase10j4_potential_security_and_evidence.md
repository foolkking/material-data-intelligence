# Phase 10J-4 Security and Evidence

Potential initialization requires a validated real scalar node field with
quantity `local_potential` or explicitly declared `electrostatic_potential`,
an approved exact unit, and strict potential-reference metadata. Generic
scalars do not receive potential claims.

Gauge formulas, interpolation, axis identities, profiles, SVG geometry, Worker,
Three.js modules, colors, and limits are application-owned. Artifacts cannot
provide code, expressions, HTML, CSS, shader, callback, URL, Worker, WASM, or
remote assets. The existing payload/decompression/voxel/mesh/PNG caps remain
active; profiles are exactly three bounded canonical axes with at most 4096
points per axis and 12288 total profile points. One linked plane mesh replaces
and disposes its predecessor.

Evidence at `docs/phase10j/evidence/phase10j4_electrostatic_potential_product/`
uses a real LOCPOT through Mock Planner, QueueWorkerRuntime, adapter persistence,
and job-scoped artifact routes. It covers source reference, cell-average and
selected-point gauges, three profiles, point difference, WebGL2 in Chromium,
Firefox, WebKit, mobile layout, performance, console, network, and screenshots.
Markers are `ELECTROSTATIC_POTENTIAL_RUNTIME_EVIDENCE_PASS`,
`ELECTROSTATIC_POTENTIAL_PRODUCT_BROWSER_EVIDENCE_PASS`,
`ELECTROSTATIC_POTENTIAL_PROFILE_EVIDENCE_PASS`,
`NO_ELECTROSTATIC_POTENTIAL_PRODUCT_EXTERNAL_NETWORK_REQUESTS`, and
`NO_SECRET_PATTERN_HITS`.
